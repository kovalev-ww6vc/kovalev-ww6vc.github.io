Privacy Policy

Kitchen Hacks Daily — last updated: 2026-11-02

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kitchen Hacks Daily application (the "App") handles information. The App is a personal-use tool that publishes content to the owner's own Pinterest Business account through the Pinterest API.

1. Who we are

Kitchen Hacks Daily is a personal content project that shares kitchen, home and storage ideas. You can contact us at kovalev-ww6vc@rambler.ru.

2. What the App does

The App connects to Pinterest through the official Pinterest API using OAuth. It creates and schedules original pins on the owner's own Pinterest Business account. It does not act on behalf of any other user.

3. Information we access

  • Basic account information of the account owner (account identifier, boards and pins owned by that account).
  • Content created by the App itself (pin images, titles and descriptions).

The App does not access the accounts, boards, pins or personal data of any other Pinterest user.

4. How we use information

Information is used solely to publish and schedule content on the owner's own account and to measure the performance of that content. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

5. Storage

Access tokens and basic statistics are stored locally on the owner's own device. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

6. Third-party services

Content published on Pinterest is subject to Pinterest's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. This site may contain affiliate links; we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.

7. Analytics

This website does not use cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

8. Children

The App and this website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact
